## Institutional Mandate
The **Akili Ecomuseum Network (AEN)** is an autonomous Digital Public Good archiving ecological health, indigenous heritage, oral histories, and environmental stewardship across an 800-kilometer continuous hydrological corridor in Kenya—from the Mount Kenya headwaters to the Lamu Archipelago.

## Licensing & Governance
All documentation, datasets, and codebase updates are published under **Creative Commons CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**. Traditional ecological knowledge and oral histories remain the perpetual cultural property of their origin communities.
